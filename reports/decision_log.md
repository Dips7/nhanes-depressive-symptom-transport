# Data decisions and amendments

15 October 2026. No predictive target-test results have been inspected.

1. Retain the planned age 18 minimum. Use an explicit `not_available_under20` category for education and marital status in every cycle. Their public adult variables are available only from age 20; do not substitute household reference-person attributes.
2. Apply the same category to smoking below age 20 in every cycle. Older adult smoking questions use age 20 eligibility, while the later cycle uses age 18. Combining youth and adult instruments would introduce inconsistent definitions. Report an age-20-plus sensitivity analysis before final interpretation.
3. Map old marital codes 1/6 to partnered, 2/3/4 to previously partnered and 5 to never married; later DMDMARTZ uses codes 1/2/3 for these groups. Codes 77/99 are missing.
4. Treat cigarette lifetime count below 100 as its own never-100-cigarettes category. Its skipped current-smoking answer is structural, not a reason to exclude the person. Never-100-cigarettes does not mean no tobacco exposure of any kind.
5. Correct the XPORT parser's tiny representation of zero before validating numeric response codes. The affected zero is approximately 5.397605e-79. Use a conservative magnitude threshold of 1e-70 only in these demographic/questionnaire/weight/anthropometry modules, and check PHQ frequencies against the official HTML tables. No original files are modified.
6. The apparent small target cell in the first failed audit was caused by that zero-decoding problem; it was not a genuine cohort property. The failed audit created no partition manifest. The corrected cohort has all 30 target design cells; no sparse-cell exclusion or allocation amendment was needed.
7. Use WTMEC2YR for the outcome's MEC setting. Store unmodified cycle weights and source weights divided by four for the four ordinary training cycles, following the CDC weighting tutorial. Do not pool 2021–2023 into source national estimates. Weight normalization for model fitting and variance replication still requires protocol freeze.
8. Target split uses deterministic hash order within each stratum/PSU cell, without labels. Save actual cell-specific updating/test inclusion fractions and adjusted partition weights, because rounding does not produce exactly 40/60 in every cell. Participants share PSUs across partitions; masked household identifiers are not available, so this is a participant split, not proof of household independence.
9. Eligibility removes people without all nine valid outcomes. Weighted prevalence is for this observed-outcome domain; no claim of complete correction for outcome nonresponse is made. Preserve all source eligibility records for later domain variance work.
10. Do not train models until the immediate protocol and novelty review are complete. Close prior work on temporal transfer and recalibration exists. A data audit is independently useful and does not commit us to an unsupported novelty claim.
11. Normalize manifest line endings to LF. An initial Windows serialization produced doubled carriage returns; participant assignments were compared and verified unchanged before rewriting, and the original serialization is retained in manifests/archive. Use the corrected file's digest for future identity checks.
12. On the user's instruction to proceed, release source-development work under protocol_v1.md while publication novelty clearance remains provisional. This refines decision 10: inaccessible conference full methods prevent a final novelty claim, not a source-only application/replication baseline. A newly identified national-survey maintenance paper establishes prior precedent for label-budget updating. Make no first-of-kind claim.
13. Freeze the expanded set as the compact variables plus BMI only, replacing the open-ended health-feature proposal. Tune source hyperparameters by mean weighted Brier over four leave-one-cycle-out folds; use 2017–2018 only for reporting after model selection. Normalize training weights to mean one for optimizer regularization. All imputation and encoding remain fold-local.
14. Fit and save compact/expanded logistic and boosted models, plus a training-prevalence reference. The compact logistic remains primary. The source executable loads only the five source eligibility tables; target eligibility is not loaded and no target prediction is generated.
15. Source validation intervals use 1000 paired Rao–Wu rescaled stratified PSU bootstrap replicates, conditional on trained source pipelines. They omit source-training uncertainty and do not resolve outcome nonresponse. Source results do not establish clinical utility. Target solver and inference implementation remain a separate pre-evaluation milestone.
16. Following the user's instructions to proceed and continue, implement updating and final inference. Preserve outcome-blind nested samples at all four budgets. Seven of 30 draws at budget 100 lack the ten-positive support minimum; no redraws are made. All primary-budget draws meet support.
17. Before final prediction, independent reader verification finds six empty-string categorical entries handled inconsistently in preparation v1. Preserve v1 and its executable, normalize empty strings to missing, record amendment protocol_target_preparation_v2.md, and repeat preparation v2 with unchanged samples. Independent features and source probabilities then agree to floating-point precision.
18. Freeze the final executable, model artifacts, protocol and partitions before target-test prediction, then evaluate once on 3277 adults. Primary inference uses 1000 jointly sampled updating/test PSU replicates, with no failed primary replicate draws. Secondary comparisons remain descriptive.

Final target results may not drive new confirmatory method or feature selection.
